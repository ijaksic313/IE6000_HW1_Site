Technical Standard Operating Procedure

xTool F1 Ultra Automated Conveyor

Hover or tap any step or bullet below to preview its mechanical motion, optical alignment, and software execution in the live monitor on the right.

Chapter 01

Safety & Operational Precautions

High-speed galvo lasers paired with conveyor indexers introduce mechanical pinch hazards and optical radiation paths:

  • E-Stop Accessibility: Maintain unhindered, rapid physical access to the red emergency stop button on the F1 Ultra chassis.
  • Pinch Point Awareness: Keep hands, cables, and tools clear of the drive rollers, idler drum, and belt edges during motor stepping.
  • Optical Radiation Shielding: When operating open pass-through feeds, wear rated OD6+ safety glasses (1064 nm and 455 nm) and attach laser shields.
  • Infeed/Outfeed Clearances: Provide at least 350 mm of clear staging at both ends to eliminate workpiece drop or binding.
Chapter 02

Hardware Docking & Mechanical Alignment

Sub-millimeter batch accuracy begins with rigid, planar mechanical registration to the F1 Ultra datum surface:

Step 1

Clean Base

Power off the machine. Remove cutting slats and wipe bed clean of grit.

Step 2

Seat Chassis

Lower conveyor frame into the base locating pins with zero axial rocking.

Step 3

Bus Cable

Plug umbilical cord into the right interior expansion port and lock.

Step 4

Check Tension

Verify 3–5 mm center span deflection; adjust end hex screws in 1/4 turns.

Chapter 03

Software Configuration (XCS)

Configure xTool Creative Space (XCS) to synchronize conveyor indexing pulses with the galvo mirror scanner:

  1. Connect F1 Ultra via USB-C or Wi-Fi and verify the device card shows Connected.
  2. Open Device Settings (Gear Icon) adjacent to the F1 Ultra device card.
  3. Under Working Mode, switch from Regular Surface to Conveyor (Auto-Stream or Long Material).
  4. Verify the on-screen feed vector arrows match physical part progression (rear infeed forward to front outfeed).
Chapter 04

Optical & Step-Distance Calibration

Calibrate hardware-to-software feed ratios to prevent longitudinal dimensional compression or stretching:

  1. Place scrap test stock onto the belt and focus using the automated dual-dot optical focus routine.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Accessory → Conveyor → Step Calibration. Fire the 100.00 mm benchmark line test.
  3. Measure distance between marks using digital calipers. Enter observed value into XCS to correct stepper multiplier.

Chapter 05

Automated Batch Processing & Smart-Fill

Deploy computer-vision driven batch production across randomized blank placement:

  • Stage Blanks: Arrange 5–15 workpieces on the belt with ≥10 mm clearance to eliminate software overlap.
  • Vision Snapshot: Click Camera Refresh to capture live part positions with the overhead sensor.
  • Smart-Fill Match: Position artwork on master part; click Smart Fill to duplicate across all detected blanks.
  • Automated Run: Press Process → Start to execute marking, auto-indexing, and continuous part ejection.
