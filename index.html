Technical SOP & Subject Matter Guide

xTool F1 Ultra Automated Conveyor

A comprehensive engineering walkthrough covering physical integration, mechanical belt tracking, optical calibration, XCS batch automation, and interactive diagnostics.

Chapter 01

Safety & Operational Precautions

Galvo-based lasers paired with automated conveyors introduce both mechanical pinch hazards and optical radiation exposure risks:

  • E-Stop Accessibility: Maintain unhindered access to the F1 Ultra red emergency stop switch at all times.
  • Pinch Point Awareness: Keep hands, cables, and loose clothing clear of drive rollers, idler drums, and belt edges during indexing.
  • Laser Radiation Protection: When operating with extended pass-through feeds, wear OD6+ rated laser safety glasses (1064 nm and 455 nm) and attach magnetic shields.
  • Infeed/Outfeed Clearances: Provide at least 350 mm of unobstructed travel at both ends to prevent part collisions or binding.
Chapter 02

Hardware Docking & Mechanical Alignment

Repeatable high-tolerance marking requires a planar, rigid connection between the conveyor frame and the F1 Ultra chassis:

Step 1

Clean & Clear Base

Power off the F1 Ultra. Remove any cutting slats or alignment jigs from the interior bed. Wipe the bed clean of debris.

Step 2

Seat Chassis

Lower the conveyor assembly straight down into the floor datum alignment holes. Ensure the chassis sits completely planar with zero wobble.

Step 3

Data Interface

Plug the multi-pin umbilical cord into the interior peripheral bus on the right chamber wall. Fasten thumbscrews securely.

Step 4

Verify Tension

Press the center span. Deflection must be 3–5 mm. Adjust end-roller tension hex bolts in 1/4-turn increments to balance tracking.

Chapter 03

Software Configuration (XCS)

Establish software communication inside xTool Creative Space (XCS) to synchronize conveyor indexing pulses:

  1. Connect the F1 Ultra via USB-C or Wi-Fi and open XCS.
  2. Select the Device Settings (Gear Icon) next to the F1 Ultra device card.
  3. Under Working Mode, select Conveyor.
  4. Select your production mode:

    • Auto-Stream / Batch Conveyor: For arrays of discrete parts (tags, keychains, coins).
    • Long Material: For continuous workpieces exceeding the galvo's native 220 × 220 mm bed area.

  5. Confirm the software feed vector matches physical part travel (rear intake to front ejection).
Chapter 04

Optical & Step-Distance Calibration

To eliminate linear compression or stretch along the indexing direction, calibrate the motor step multiplier:

  1. Place a flat scrap test substrate (such as cardboard or an anodized aluminum sheet) onto the belt surface.
  2. Run the auto-focus routine to establish accurate focal height.
  3. In XCS, navigate to Settings → Accessory → Conveyor → Step Calibration.
  4. Set the test target distance to 100.00 mm and click Mark Benchmark Line. The laser marks a transverse reference line, indexes the conveyor, and marks a second parallel line.
  5. Measure the exact center-to-center distance between both marks using digital calipers. Enter the measured length into XCS to auto-correct the feed ratio.
Chapter 05

Automated Batch Processing & Smart-Fill

Execute automated part production utilizing the overhead machine vision camera:

  • Stage Blanks: Place 5–15 identical blanks onto the conveyor belt with at least 10 mm separation.
  • Vision Snapshot: Click Camera Refresh in XCS to capture the live physical coordinates of all pieces.
  • Configure Master Element: Drag your vector graphic or text over the first detected blank to set position, power, and speed.
  • Smart Batch Match: Highlight the master design and click Smart Fill. The software automatically detects centroids and duplicates the design across every blank.
  • Process Execution: Click Process → Start to begin automatic marking and indexing.
